# start | Getting started
Choose where you start: open the BeamNG.Earth map, find anywhere on Earth, such as your home town, and teleport there. Then press Start here: your starter car is waiting.
Fit a radiator first: the starter car needs one. Press Buy parts on the Garage tab, fit one and pay there.
Then fit cargo boxes: the starter car only has its boot, which takes 2 slots. In Buy parts, put Cargo Load boxes in the rear seat slot to start. More boxes carry bigger loads, and bigger loads pay more.
Drive to a town's depot, a tin shed with a computer on a desk in front. Park close to the computer, get out, walk up to it and press Use computer. Pick a job and press Take job: it fills your car.
Follow the GPS to the drop-off, stop in the town and press Deliver to get paid in full.
The game saves itself when you stop after a delivery, a purchase, a repair or a relocation. Press Save to keep anything else.

# jobs | Jobs and pay
Every town's jobs are on its Town tab, and you take them at its depot's computer. Each job shows where it goes, how far and what it pays.
Green pay is better than usual, red is worse. A shortage pays double, and a jackpot pays even more.
Short jobs are up to 8 km, medium 8 to 13 km and long 13 to 300 km. Pick one with the buttons next to Jobs.
Every board also has a long haul of parts or bricks to a town {longHaulMinKm} to {longHaulMaxKm} km away, for bigger pay.
Too big for your car? Use the Load slider on a job to take part of it, for part of the pay.
Rough roads and steep ground pay more. The number next to the town's name shows how much, such as ×2.7.
Tap a job to see its details and press GPS to see the route before you take it.

# depot | Depots and the computer
Every town has a depot: a tin shed with a computer on a desk in front of it, near the middle of town. A fuel depot has one too.
Jobs, off-road drops and fuel runs are only taken at the depot's computer. The Town tab shows them, and how far away the depot is.
In town, an arrow on the Town tab points to the depot and says how far it is. Press GPS next to it for a route to the door. The minimised HUD shows the distance too.
Park within {depotTruckMetres} m of the computer, get out and walk up to it. Press Use computer when it shows, pick a job and take it.
Your truck must still be within {depotTruckMetres} m when you take the job, or it won't load.
Deliveries haven't changed: drive to the drop-off and press Deliver on the HUD.
Depots go in a real open car park near the middle of town where OpenStreetMap shows one, or else on clear ground beside a road.
If there's no clear ground for a shed, the depot is just the desk and computer, at street level by the road.
If no spot is found within {depotGiveUpSeconds} seconds of reaching town, the desk and computer go down beside the nearest road anyway, so a town's jobs are never locked for long.
The depot appears a few seconds after you reach a town, once its buildings have loaded. If a building loads late where the depot stands, the depot moves to a clear spot as soon as you're not standing at it.
The shed, desk and computer are scenery only: you can walk and drive through them.
A depot is taken down once you're about {depotFarKm} km away, and put back when you return. A depot left over from an earlier version of the mod is cleared away on its own.

# route | On the road
The GPS is set when you take a job. If it goes missing, copy the drop-off coordinates on the Route tab into the BeamNG.Earth map.
Nearby on the Route tab lists the closest towns. Their dots show what each one pays well for, and GPS takes you there.
Some jobs have no road at the end: follow the arrow on the Route tab and stop close to the spot.
Abandoning a job costs {abandonFee}. Teleporting without a booking cancels your job for the same fee.

# fuel | Fuel runs and tankers
Fuel runs are long hauls of fuel, {fuelRunMinKm} to {fuelRunMaxKm} km, from a depot in a quiet regional town out to a country petrol station. The further you go, the more they pay.
You'll find them on the Town tab. Teleport takes you to the depot for free. Load them at the depot's computer, like town jobs.
They need a tank. Fit a Cargo Load fluid tank with Buy parts, or tow a tanker trailer. A tank also lets you take water jobs.

# car | Your car, parts and cargo
Buy parts, Buy new car and Second hand all work the same way: change what you like, then the bill shows on the Garage tab. Nothing is paid until you press Pay, and you can undo it.
Your cargo space is counted in slots and kg. More Cargo Load boxes carry bigger loads, and bigger loads pay more.
You can change parts with a job on board, even on a fuel run. Only taking off the cargo boxes or tank your load is in waits until you deliver.
Second hand cars look worn but drive like new, and cost less.
After the parts menu, a teleport or coming back from the helicopter, you're put back on the camera you last used in that car. If you were on the free camera yourself, you stay on it.

# cars | Your cars and trailers
You start with {startSlots} car slots. A base or a corp gives you {baseSlots}, and then you can buy a {boughtSlots}th for {slotPrice}.
Drive brings a parked car to you anywhere for {driveFee} and parks the one you are in. Each car keeps its own parts, fuel and damage.
Sell pays {sellShare} of a car's worth, less any repair.
A trailer adds cargo space. Each load is shared between your car and the trailer. You need the right hitch on your car: fit one with Buy parts.
Back up slowly to a trailer and it hitches itself. It only adds space once it is hitched.
Trailers come with their boxes or tanks fitted. For more room, buy a bigger trailer. You can own as many trailers as you have car slots.

# repair | Repairs, towing, insurance and fuel
Press Get repair quote on the Garage tab, then Repair to pay and fix it. Repairs at your base are {baseRepairOff} off.
Stuck? Press Get out, walk to a clear, flat spot within {towRange} m of your car and press Tow car here. You keep your job. Towing doesn't fix damage.
After a serious crash you can claim insurance: your car is fixed and put back on the road, but the job is lost. A claim costs at least {insuranceMin}, and more the richer you are.
If you're short, what you owe comes out of your next deliveries, {debtShare} of each, and buying is off until it's paid.
Fuel is cheapest at a petrol station: drive slowly up to a pump. Refuelling anywhere else costs {townFuelTimes} times the pump price.

# relocate | Relocate
Move your empty car anywhere in the world. Pick a region, find a town or paste coordinates, pay, then press Teleport.
Your car goes with you; cargo can't. Teleporting without a booking cancels your job for {abandonFee}, and pay stops until you go back.
Now and then a town in a tough region offers to pay your relocation back if you come and deliver there.

# heli | Heli and beacons
Hire a Bell 407 for {heliFee} to fly anywhere and scout towns and their jobs. The hire lasts until you go back to your car.
Your car waits where you left it, with its job, fuel and damage. A heli crash costs {heliCrash} and ends the hire.
Fly high for a while and you may pick up a "Don't ask questions" beacon worth {beaconPay}. There's no GPS: follow the arrow and stop at the spot.
A crash loses the beacon's package, and an insurance claim then costs at least {beaconInsurance}.
The helicopter is a free mod from the BeamNG forum. The Heli tab says which one and where to get it.

# base | Bases, corps and turf
Buy a base where you are parked for {basePrice}. Around its town grows a circle: your turf.
Jobs that start or end in your turf pay you more. Your circle grows as you get richer, and never shrinks.
Your base keeps a trailer for you, and repairs there are {baseRepairOff} off.
Online, other truckers pay a {toll} toll when they deliver in your turf, and you pay one in theirs. The job board shows it first.
Online, others can ask to join your base as your corp. Members get the turf bonus and pay no toll there, and the tolls are shared.

# robbers | Robbers and the EMP
On a job of {robberMinKm} km or more, a road robber may hit you with an EMP and take some of your money. The bigger the job, the more they take.
Chase them: get within {empRange} m and fire your own EMP to get it back, plus a bonus from their trunk. If they get too far ahead, they escape.
A delivery is only ever robbed once.
In single player you can switch robbers and base thieves off with the Robbers and thieves tick box on the Route tab. It costs nothing and you can switch them back on any time. Online, every trucker can be robbed.
Your EMP takes {empSeconds} seconds to charge again after each shot.
A base thief can strike about once every {thiefMinutes} minutes of play, when you're at least {thiefAwayKm} km from your base, and take some of your money. Chase takes you there for free. Catch them for your money back plus a bonus from their trunk.
On a chase, your job waits where you left it, with its cargo. Your trailer waits at your base and comes back behind you, still loaded.
To chase in another car, press Chase in and pick one of your parked cars. It's free. Your truck is parked in that car's slot with your job in it, and Back to your job swaps it back in for you.
Robbers never come during a Mayday Rescue run. A thief who strikes before one waits for you, and their Chase clock stops until the run is over.

# mayday | Mayday Rescue | online
Now and then a Mayday call goes out somewhere on Earth. The first trucker to reach the scene wins the reward.
Pick an edge of the circle to start from, free, with an empty car. There's no GPS: head off road and follow the arrow.
Any teleport, the heli or changing cars ends your run, but you can start again from any edge.

# online | Playing online and the land race | online
Online, everyone trucks on the same Earth. Your single player career stays on this PC.
The server gives you its own copy of the mod. Your copy steps aside while you're on, and is turned back on when you leave.
Each HUD layout has its own apps, and a server usually shows a different layout from single player. The first time the HUD is missing, a pop-up offers to add it: press Add the HUD.
Bases grow into turf, and the first corp to cover enough of Earth's land wins the season. Then everyone starts again.
The season leaderboard ranks everyone by what they're worth.
Follow the race on the live server page, on your phone or a second screen. Its address is at the top of this page.

# extras | Street View, sightseeing and radio
Street View and the radio open in your web browser and follow your truck. Play BeamNG in Borderless or Windowed mode to switch with Alt+Tab.
Street View's own settings, like pop out, zoom and how often it updates, are on its page in the browser.
The radio finds live stations around you and can switch to a nearby one as you drive. Play, Stop, Next station and the volume are on its page. Stations come from Radio Browser, a free list of internet radio, and use about 60 MB of internet an hour.
Go sightseeing saves your game and swaps your truck for a free car, so you can teleport anywhere and look around. Return to your job brings it all back.
