How to play
  1. Go to {{data.startTown}}: copy the coordinates, paste them into the BeamNG.Earth map and teleport there. Your starter car is waiting.
  2. Fit a radiator first: the starter car needs one. Use the parts menu (Ctrl+W) and pay on the Garage tab.
  3. Stop in a town. On the Town tab pick a good, set how far to drive with the slider, and press Buy next to a drop-off.
  4. Follow the GPS to the drop-off, stop in the town and press Deliver to get paid.
  5. Refuel on the Town tab. Repairs, parts and bigger cars are on the Garage tab.
  6. To go far, book shipping on the Garage tab.
  7. Press Save often. Anything since your last Save is lost when you exit.

Tips
  • Buy a load in one town and deliver it to another. Green dots are cheap to buy, red ones pay well. The best run line suggests one.
  • Buying a load sets the BeamNG.Earth GPS to the drop-off, so the route and arrows show up on their own.
  • Deliveries must be driven: a load only sells once you have driven most of the way there. A teleport longer than 2 km loses the load and its money; a short hop past a cliff or river is fine. To go far, book shipping on the Garage tab.
  • Weight is real: a heavy load is slower, brakes later and burns more fuel. Bricks run out of weight before space.
  • Rough driving damages the cargo and cuts the payout. Parts are the most fragile.
  • Fuel costs money. Refuel on the Town tab, stopped in a town.
  • Steep, remote and high regions pay more, up to ×3: the boost shows next to the region name. Flat Sydney pays ×1, the Blue Mountains about ×1.5 and Death Road in Bolivia ×3. Goods cost more to buy there too.
  • Save often. Money, loads and parts since your last Save are lost when you exit or load the level again.
  • Repairs cost more on pricier cars. Keep some money back.
