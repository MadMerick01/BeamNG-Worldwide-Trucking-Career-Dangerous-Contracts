Tips
  • Take a job in one town and deliver it to another. A job fills all your cargo slots, so bigger boxes and vehicles earn more. Parts take 1 slot (15 kg) each and bricks 3 slots (150 kg).
  • Every town also posts a water or fuel job. Fluids need a fluid tank: fit a Cargo Load fluid tank in the parts menu and pay for it on the Garage tab. They pay about a quarter more than bricks for the weight.
  • Taking a job sets the BeamNG.Earth GPS to the drop-off, so the route and arrows show up on their own.
  • Every town also has an off-road drop: a spot 2 to 3 km out with no roads and no GPS. Follow the arrow on the Route tab and stop within 100 m of it. It pays like a 5 km job, more in steep country. The spot is random, so it may be up a hill or across a creek: that's the challenge.
  • Jobs must be driven: a job only delivers once you have driven most of the way there. A teleport longer than 2 km cancels it; a short hop past a cliff or river is fine. To go far, book shipping on the Garage tab.
  • Each delivery makes that town want the good a little less for an hour or so. Try another town or another good.
  • Watch for gold dots: a shortage pays double for an hour, and the odd jackpot job pays two to three times.
  • Weight is real: a heavy load is slower, brakes later and burns more fuel. Bricks run out of weight before space.
  • After a serious crash you can claim insurance on the Garage tab: $1,000, the car is fixed and back on the road, but the job is lost.
  • Fuel costs money. Refuel on the Town tab, stopped in a town.
  • Rough roads pay more, up to ×2: the bonus shows next to the region name. Flat Sydney pays ×1, the Blue Mountains about ×1.25 and Death Road in Bolivia ×2.
  • Save often. Money, jobs and parts since your last Save are lost when you exit or load the level again.
  • Repairs cost more on pricier cars. Keep some money back.
