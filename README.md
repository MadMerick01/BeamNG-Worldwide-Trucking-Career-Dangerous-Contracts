# BeamNG Worldwide Trucking Career: Dangerous Contracts

**Haul cargo anywhere on Earth. Start in a rust bucket with no radiator or front brakes**
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_CPI1Gn0XDQ

Join the Discord for bug reports, ideas and updates: https://discord.gg/eYJCfXNGbP

A freight career for [BeamNG.Earth](https://github.com/TecnaGamer/BeamNG.Earth), the real-world map for BeamNG.drive.

## What you need

- **BeamNG.drive 0.40.**
- **[BeamNG.Earth](https://github.com/TecnaGamer/BeamNG.Earth)**, installed and working. Follow its own install guide first. The mod's **Teleport** buttons use Earth's teleport, so keep Earth's companion app running too. Without it you can still paste coordinates into Earth's map yourself.
- Optional, for heli flights: the **[Revamped Bell 407](https://www.beamng.com/threads/revamped-bell-407-with-weapons.98195/)** mod by stefan750.

## Features

- **178,700 real earth towns, each with a job board.** Move parts, bricks, water and fuel.
- **Garage - Build your Rig.** Upgrade parts, buy and sell **new** and **second hand** vehicles.
- **Trailers.** Buy one of 16 trailers, from a small box trailer to a 34,000 L tanker, with cargo boxes or fluid tanks fitted. Hitch it for extra cargo space. Your base of operations has a spot for one trailer, so with a base you can own two; without one, a depot keeps your trailer for a fee.
- **Real businesses.** Many town jobs end at a real shop, yard or warehouse in that town, found on OpenStreetMap. Pull up within 25 m of it to deliver; with Street View open you see the real place.
- **Off-road drops.** No GPS route: follow the arrow on the Route tab to a spot off the road.
- **Dangerous highly paid contracts.** Death Road, Carhuaz, Anta, Marcará, Zoji La, Ravin de Piquet, Sani Pass and Grimsel, for up to **8x the pay**. They stay in rotation after you deliver them, so you can drive your favourites again.
- **Dynamic economy.** Towns want different goods, repeat runs pay less, shortages pay double.
- **Steep, remote and notorious roads** pay up to 3x. Regions are graded for difficulty.
- **Base of operations.** For $250,000, buy a base in any town on the **Base** tab. A circle of turf grows around it as you get richer, and jobs that start or end in your turf pay up to 20% more. Your base keeps a second car and a second trailer you can swap into there, and repairs at your base are half price. **Open base map** shows the circles on a real map in your web browser.
- **Relocate.** Pay to move to any town or coordinates on Earth. Some of the world's hardest regions will pay your way if you come and deliver their wares.
- **Fuel, punctures and breakdowns.** Refuel in towns, repair burst tyres and use **Tow to road** when you're stuck.
- **Insurance** after a big crash costs $1,000 when you're starting out, but covers less the richer you get: at $250,000 of wealth a claim costs half the repair, and from $500,000 the whole repair. The load is lost. If you can't afford the claim, the rest becomes an insurance debt: half of every delivery's pay goes to it until it's paid, or press **Pay off** on the Garage tab, and you can't buy parts or cars while you owe it. Abandoning a job costs $1,000, and the Insert key won't fix your car for free.
- **Saves itself.** Your progress saves every 5 minutes, the next time you stop, as well as when you press **Save**. Press the **–** at the top of the HUD to tuck it away while you drive.
- **Street View window.** On the HUD's **Street View** tab, press **Turn on** to follow your truck in Google Street View in your web browser, updated every 10 seconds (or every 6 or 20, in the page's Settings). Handy on a second screen.
- **Go sightseeing.** Save, swap your truck for a free Moonhawk, teleport anywhere on Earth to look around, then press **Return to your job** to get your truck and job back.
- **Heli flights.** Hire a Bell 407 for $1,000 and search for **'dont ask questions'** jobs. Needs the [Revamped Bell 407](https://www.beamng.com/threads/revamped-bell-407-with-weapons.98195/) mod by stefan750.

## Install

1. Download `worldwide-trucking-dangerous-contracts.zip` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Drop it, **still zipped**, into the `mods` folder of your BeamNG user folder. If an older `earth-freight-sim.zip` or `earth_freight.zip` is there, delete it so two copies don't load. Your saves carry over.
3. In game, turn on the **BeamNG Worldwide Trucking Career: Dangerous Contracts** HUD app. Go to System >  HUD Apps > .Earth (edit layout) > +Add app > Scroll down to bottom of HUD APPS >
   BeamNG Worldwide Trucking Career: Dangerous Contracts > + Add to layout > (move it where you wish onscreen) > Save

## How to Start

1. Load the BeamNG.Earth Freeroam level and type your name. Open the BeamNG.Earth map, teleport anywhere on Earth, such as your home town, and press **Start here**. You start there with a rusty Burnside and $6,000.
2. Fit a radiator first: **Buy parts** on the Garage tab. The starter car needs one.
3. Then fit cargo boxes. The starter car only has its boot (2 slots), so in **Buy parts** put **Cargo Load** boxes in the rear seat slot to start. More boxes carry bigger loads, and bigger loads pay more.
4. Stop in any town, open the **Town** tab, pick a job and press **Take job**. The GPS is set for you.
5. Drive it there in one piece and press **Deliver**.
6. Your progress saves itself every 5 minutes, and you can press **Save** any time. Next time, **Teleport to last save** puts you right back.


## Multiplayer

Drive the career with friends on a [BeamMP](https://beammp.com/) server running BeamNG.Earth. The **Play online** card on the start page shows whether the server is up and how many truckers are on it, with buttons for the live career page and the Discord.

- **Your own career on the server.** The server keeps it, apart from your single-player **Offline career**, and saves are only marked as saved once the server has them. Players only need BeamNG.Earth's app: the server sends them the mod. If you have your own copy of the mod turned on, the server asks you to turn it off so everyone plays the same build. You still add the HUD app once (see Install, step 3); the server reminds you in chat.
- **Bases and turf.** Everyone sees everyone's bases and circles on the **Base** tab and the Base map. A line under the town name on the HUD always says whose turf you're in. Deliver in someone else's turf and 20% of the pay goes to them as a toll; deliver in your own and you get up to 20% more. Circles start at 800 km and grow with your wealth, up to 1,600 km.
- **Corps.** Press **Ask to join** on another trucker's base to join their corp, up to 5 truckers. Members pay no toll in their corp's turf, get up to 10% more there, keep a car and trailer at the corp yard, and share the corp's tolls equally. Once a corp's newest base is full size, any member can open another base for free.
- **The land race.** Each season, the first trucker or corp whose turf covers half of Earth's land wins. After 12 weeks, whoever holds the most land wins. A land meter on the HUD, the Base tab and the web page shows who's leading. Then everyone starts over.
- **Ship Day.** Every day a ship docks at a real port with 5 containers of high-value cargo. Race to the port and take one from the Ship Day card on the **Town** tab.
- **While you were away.** When you come back, a card on the HUD shows the tolls paid to you while you were gone and any corp news.
- **Live career page.** The [server's web page](https://madmerick01.github.io/BeamNG-Worldwide-Trucking-Career-Dangerous-Contracts) shows who's driving and where, every base's turf on a map, the season leaderboard ranked by wealth, and past season winners.

To host a server, download `worldwide-trucking-server.zip` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest) and follow [MULTIPLAYER-SETUP.md](docs/MULTIPLAYER-SETUP.md). To keep it up to date, see [SERVER-UPDATES.md](docs/SERVER-UPDATES.md).

## Updating

Download the new `worldwide-trucking-dangerous-contracts.zip`, delete the old one from your `mods` folder and drop the new one in. Your saves carry over unless the changelog says otherwise.

### Settings

Optional: `/settings/earthFreight/config.json` in your BeamNG user folder (see `mod/earth_freight/config.example.json`).

- `base`: a fixed spot every new game starts at, instead of the one you choose with **Start here**.
- `autoGps`: `false` stops the mod setting BeamNG.Earth's GPS when you take a job.

### Development

```
python -m pip install -r tests/requirements.txt
python tests/run.py                    # every Lua, HUD and packaging test
python tools/package.py                # builds dist/worldwide-trucking-dangerous-contracts.zip
python tools/make_places.py            # rebuilds the worldwide place tiles from tools/data/
python tools/make_land.py              # rebuilds the land map the server's land race measures turf against
python tools/make_places.py --report   # prints the pay boost of some well-known places
python tools/sample_terrain.py --places PLACES --cache DIR   # re-samples elevation (see its --help)
python tools/check_earth_versions.py C:\path\to\BeamNG.Earth   # checks the adapter against Earth releases
```

Every pull request adds a line for players under **Coming next** in [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md); see [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md).
